Services

Three ways to work with us

Permanent

Contingency recruitment

Our standard model. No placement, no fee. Fees are banded by salary, charged on basic salary rather than OTE and agreed in writing before we begin.

Senior hires

Retained search

For director and senior leadership roles. A commitment fee at instruction and the balance on placement, with a confidential, fully managed search.

Flexible

Temporary and contract

Cover for peaks, projects and absences, with a clear and fair route if you want to make a temporary worker permanent.

How we work

Two weeks from first conversation to placement

Six clear stages. Our target is two weeks, with the pace set by your interview availability and how quickly decisions are made.

  1. 01
    Day 1

    Briefing call

    The role, your team, your culture and what has not worked before.

  2. 02
    Days 1 to 5

    Active sourcing

    Targeted search, headhunting, referrals and advertising.

  3. 03
    Days 3 to 7

    Screening

    Salary, notice, motivation, fit and the competencies you need.

  4. 04
    Week 1

    Submission packs

    Written packs, not loose CVs, so you decide in minutes.

  5. 05
    Week 2

    Interviews

    We schedule, confirm and prepare every candidate for your format.

  6. 06
    Offer

    Offer and placement

    We manage the offer, handle counteroffers and stay close to the start.

Aftercare

The placement is not the finish line

Most agencies disappear once the invoice is raised. We stay close, because a hire only counts if it works.

01

Offer and counteroffer

We handle the offer conversation and protect the hire if a counteroffer appears.

02

Pre-start onboarding

Your new starter gets start details, first-week expectations and a day one checklist.

03

Client handover pack

Employment details, right to work confirmation and references in one place.

04

Week 1 check-in

We speak to you and your new starter at the end of the first week.

05

Month 1 check-in

A second conversation, so anything small is caught early.

06

3-month guarantee

If your hire leaves or is dismissed for performance within three months, we find you a replacement.
