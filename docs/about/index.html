About us

We are not a volume agency.
We never will be.

Live 2 Help Recruitment was built on a simple belief: the right hire is not about matching a CV to a job description. It is about understanding what your business actually needs, and finding the person who delivers it from day one.

Our story

Founder-led and deliberately boutique

We have been trading for over nine years, recruiting across engineering, procurement, commercial, technical and industrial sectors across the UK.

Being small is a choice. Your brief gets personal attention from people who understand your sector, rather than a bulk send to an unqualified list. You always know exactly who is working on your role.

We have also built our own tools around the way we work: a free Hiring Toolkit for employers, a Talent Suite that helps candidates get seen, and the systems behind every submission pack and prep pack we produce.

Our values

Open doors
